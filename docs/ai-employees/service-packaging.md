# AI Employee Service Packaging

This is a future freelance/service packaging model, not current SaaS billing scope.

## Offer ladder

```text
1. Role / Workflow Audit
2. AI Employee Blueprint
3. Shadow Pilot
4. Supervised Deployment
5. Bounded Active Deployment
6. Managed AI Employee Retainer
7. Additional Roles / Workflow Expansion
```

## 1. Role / Workflow Audit

Map:

- responsibilities;
- repetitive tasks;
- systems;
- decisions;
- exceptions;
- approvals;
- data;
- current time/cost;
- business outcomes.

The goal is not to automate the entire human job.

The goal is to identify work units.

## 2. AI Employee Blueprint

Deliver:

- Role Brief;
- task decomposition;
- deterministic vs AI vs agent vs human classification;
- authority matrix;
- skills/tools/workflows;
- knowledge/memory policy;
- risk model;
- evaluation plan;
- deployment recommendation;
- ROI baseline.

## 3. Shadow Pilot

The role processes representative work without independently causing material production side effects.

Compare against current human/process outcomes.

## 4. Supervised Deployment

Use real work with human review at defined points.

Collect correction and escalation patterns.

## 5. Bounded Active Deployment

Expand autonomy only for action classes with evidence.

## 6. Managed Retainer

Potential service:

- monitoring;
- failure/incident handling;
- evaluation regression;
- provider/tool changes;
- prompt/role-version maintenance;
- monthly outcome/ROI report;
- authority review;
- knowledge updates;
- agreed workflow improvements.

## Pricing principle

Do not base value only on “hours of AI.”

Possible value anchors:

- time/cycle reduction;
- volume handled;
- error/rework reduction;
- business outcome;
- service/support responsibility.

Actual commercial pricing is outside the repository spec until the business model is tested.

## Client expectation

Never sell “replace an entire employee instantly.”

Sell a bounded role/outcome with explicit capabilities, limitations, human ownership, and measurable pilot criteria.
